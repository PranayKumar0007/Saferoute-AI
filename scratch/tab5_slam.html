2. Justification of 7 CEP (Complex Engineering Problem) Characteristics

  • P1 (Depth of Knowledge): Implemented first-principles Haversine distance, orthogonal line-segment projection, and supervised ensemble ML classification without black-box APIs.
  • P2 (Conflicting Requirements): Solved the classic dilemma between High Sensitivity (catching detours) and False Alarms (traffic light stops) via 3-window debounce and 15s in-app verification.
  • P3 (Depth of Analysis): Performed multi-model cross-validation benchmarking (Logistic Regression vs Decision Tree vs Random Forest) and systematic feature group ablation.
  • P4 (Familiarity of Issues): Handled real-world transit telemetry challenges: GPS drift, late-night transit risks, and unscheduled breakdown dwell times.
  • P5 (Extent of Applicable Codes): Followed standard PEP 8, IEEE 830 software engineering specifications, and mobile privacy principles.
  • P6 (Extent of Stakeholder Involvement): Addressed diverse needs of passengers (control & privacy), parents (actionable GPS links), and municipal authorities (anonymized safety heatmaps).
  • P7 (Interdependence of Components): Architected 5 coupled modules with defined JSON interface contracts and live ms latency profiling.
