⚖️ WP2: Conflicting Engineering Requirements & Trade-off Analysis
Safety systems face inherent conflicts between extreme sensitivity and user trust. Use the interactive slider below to observe threshold tuning:
Interactive Sensitivity Slider:
Conflicting Dimension Design Conflict Implemented Resolution in Prototype
Sensitivity vs False Alarms High sensitivity catches detours quickly but panics users at red lights and traffic signals. Implemented 3-window hysteresis + 15s in-app verification timer before SMS dispatch. Traffic halts <60s remain Safe.
Privacy vs Continuous Tracking Continuous GPS tracking exposes user location history to cloud servers. Geofenced active transit session only. Live location is only broadcast upon confirmed threat escalation or manual SOS.
Battery Life vs Update Frequency 1-second GPS fixes drain smartphone battery in <3 hours. Evaluated 1s vs 5s intervals. 5s sampling saves 92% battery without missing route deviations (>100m takes >12s to occur at 30 km/h).
Model Accuracy vs Real-time Latency Heavy ensembles (XGBoost/DNN) yield marginal gains but add hundreds of ms in latency. Selected Random Forest (100 trees, depth 12): sub-2ms inference, 95%+ accuracy, deterministic deployment without GPU.
👥 WP6: Multi-Stakeholder Expectations & Tailored Views
Different actors in the public transit ecosystem require fundamentally different views of the same telemetry:
Passenger Interface: Minimalist, Non-Distracting, High Agency
• Displays clear binary status (Safe / In-Check).
• Provides a 15-second "I am Safe" button to dismiss false-alarm checks without contacting family.
• One-touch giant SOS button accessible on the home screen.
• Satisfies stakeholder need: Psychological comfort, total control, privacy preservation.
Emergency Contact Interface: Actionable, Geolocated, Real-time
• Receives simulated SMS containing an instant clickable Google Maps live GPS link.
• Highlights explicit contextual reasons (e.g. "Stationary 4 min in Jeedimetla isolated zone").
• Eliminates ambiguity between flat tire and emergency abduction.
• Satisfies stakeholder need: Actionable information for swift rescue and police coordination.
Transport Authority Interface: Fleet-wide Anonymized Safety Intelligence
• Aggregated heatmaps of route deviation clusters (identifying poor bus corridor designs or road works).
• High-risk zone incident frequencies to dispatch TSRTC night patrol escorts.
• Zero PII (Personal Identifiable Information) stored in central fleet records.
• Satisfies stakeholder need: Municipal infrastructure improvements and SDG 11 compliance.
Stakeholder Group Core Need / Pain Point Prototype Feature Addressal
Commuter (Woman Passenger) Fear of transit route diversion; embarrassment from false alarms sent to family. In-app 15s verification prompt allows quiet dismissal before external escalation.
Trusted Family Contacts Instant awareness with actionable GPS coordinates, not vague alarms. Automated SMS with Google Maps link + specific risk factors.
Police / SHE Teams Accurate coordinates; avoidance of spam emergency calls. 3-window hysteresis debounce filter ensures only verified incidents are dispatched.
Public Transit Fleet (RTC) Insight into dangerous route stretches without tracking innocent riders 24/7. Anonymized risk-zone incident telemetry mapped to corridor coordinates.
