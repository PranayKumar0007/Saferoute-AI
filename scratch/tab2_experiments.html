📐 Mathematical Derivations from First Principles
1. Haversine Great-Circle Formula
a = sin²(Δφ/2) + cos(φ₁)cos(φ₂)sin²(Δλ/2)
c = 2 · atan2(√a, √(1−a))
d = R · c (R = 6,371,000 m)
Accounts for spherical Earth curvature without planar distortion across transit routes.
2. Instantaneous Velocity
v = (d_haversine / Δt) × 3.6 [km/h]
Var(v) = (1/N) Σ (v_i − μ_v)²
Sliding window mean and variance to identify erratic speed spikes and prolonged zero-speed halts.
3. Point-to-Polyline Projection
t = clamp((P − A) · (B − A) / |B − A|², 0, 1)
C = A + t · (B − A)
Deviation = Haversine(P, C)
Determines perpendicular Euclidean offset from planned route line segment.
